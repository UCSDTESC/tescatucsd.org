Student Travel Fund

The Student Travel Fund supports Jacobs School of Engineering undergraduates who attend conferences and competitions. It covers travel expenses such as transportation and hotel costs, as well as conference or registration fees. This year, TESC manages the application process and reviews applications.

Apply for funding Read the guidelines

This is a reimbursement program, so students pay their expenses first and then submit receipts. Typical reimbursements range from $300 to $500, depending on documented expenses and available funds. TESC does not screen applicants during the summer, so students traveling over the summer should apply before the end of the previous academic year.

How it works

  1. 1

    Complete the Google Form linked on the funding page. Include a public Google Drive link to your completed Student Certification for Business Travel form.

  2. 2

    TESC reviews applications on a rolling basis, which takes three to four weeks. Leave at least four weeks before your travel. If you do not receive an email within three weeks, follow up with the board by email.

  3. 3

    Approved applicants receive an email with a link to the Post-Travel Form.

  4. 4

    Submit the Post-Travel Form within two weeks of travel, with itemized receipts and proof of payment for every expense. TESC does not process requests received after this deadline, and the award is forfeited.
